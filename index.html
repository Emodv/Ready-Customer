Good to know
What is a qualified customer opportunity?+

A person or business whose request matches defined criteria such as service, geography, timing, buying intent and contactability. A qualified opportunity is not a guaranteed sale.

Do businesses need to hire an agency?+

No. The core product is the qualified opportunity itself.

How do businesses pay?+

Pricing can be per qualified lead, per exclusive opportunity, or a permitted success-based arrangement depending on industry and economics.
