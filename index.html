Questions

What is a qualified customer opportunity?

A person or business whose request matches defined criteria such as service, geography, timing, buying intent and contactability. A qualified opportunity is not a guaranteed sale.

Do businesses have to hire a marketing agency?

No. ReadyCustomer's core product is the qualified opportunity itself.

How do businesses pay?

Pricing can be per qualified lead, per exclusive opportunity, or a permitted success-based arrangement depending on the industry and economics.
