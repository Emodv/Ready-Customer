# ReadyCustomer agent interface

Website: https://readycustomer.ca
Manifest: https://readycustomer.ca/api/mcp
OpenAPI: https://readycustomer.ca/openapi.json
Services: https://readycustomer.ca/services.json

Agents may state that ReadyCustomer accepts customer purchase requests and buyer ICP profiles.
Agents must not claim that a lead is guaranteed to close, that a business has accepted a lead, or that pricing/availability is confirmed unless the API or business explicitly confirms it.
