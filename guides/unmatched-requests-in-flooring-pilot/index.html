Unmatched requests in the flooring pilot

Requests that do not match the current automated pilot criteria are stored. They are not routed to businesses while matching remains flooring-only in GTA/Ontario.

Current pilot scope

Automated matching is limited to flooring requests that meet the pilot filters: valid contact, consent, GTA/Ontario geography, timing within 60 days, and a fit score of at least 70. Flooring budgets below CAD $2,500 are typically disqualified when a budget is supplied.

What happens to other requests

Non-flooring requests and requests outside the GTA/Ontario service area are still stored. They remain unmatched. They are not offered to participating businesses under the current v0.2 matching rules.

This keeps the pilot focused while data accumulates on acceptance and outcomes. Other categories listed in services.json are noted as pilot but are not automatically routed.

Why store unmatched requests

  • To capture real demand signals that can inform future category expansion.
  • To avoid discarding information that may become relevant if rules change.
  • To keep the qualification and matching logic transparent.

No claim is made that stored unmatched requests will be routed later or that they represent sales opportunities today.

For businesses

Participating businesses define their own ICP. During the pilot, only qualifying flooring requests that match the stated criteria are routed exclusively. Response speed, quote quality and the customer’s decision still determine outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

Are unmatched requests deleted?

No. They are stored and marked unmatched. They are not routed under the current flooring pilot rules.

Will other categories be matched automatically?

Not in the current v0.2 release. Expansion depends on observed outcomes and explicit rule updates. No timeline is promised.

Does storage mean a business will see the request?

No. Unmatched means it is not offered under the pilot matching process.
